  description: "Step into the stylized world of one of Hong Kong cinema's most iconic auteurs. This retrospective celebrates the bold and diverse filmography of Johnnie To, whose work transcends genres—from the gripping intensity of Election to the operatic crime saga A Hero Never Dies, and from the poignant romance of Blind Detective and the dazzling corporate satire of Office.
