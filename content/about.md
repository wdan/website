content_paragraphs:
  - "BACCS Cinema transforms how San Francisco Bay Area communities experience Asian storytelling. We are not only a film curation and exhibition platform rooted in local communities, but also a builder of local film industry ecosystems. We curate and present exceptional Asian cinema that sparks conversation, builds understanding, and bridges cultures and communities."
  - "Since 2023, we've partnered with theaters, film festivals, arts institutions, and streaming platforms throughout the West Coast to bring audiences everything from restored classics to contemporary independent films, from director retrospectives to cross-disciplinary themed series, revealing the full spectrum of Asian cinematic expression. Each screening and post-film discussion becomes a moment of connecting, understanding, and imagination through cinematic stories."
  - "BACCS Cinema continues to bring a wider range of powerful Asian films to the cultural centers in North America. Meanwhile, our commitment extends beyond exhibition. We actively invest in local Asian filmmakers and support independent projects, ensuring these vital voices reach wider audiences."
  - "BACCS Cinema believes in the power of film to bridge divides and deepen empathy. Join us in creating a cultural space where every story matters and every voice is heard."
